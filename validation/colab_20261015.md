What this settles:

- The record recipe does not reproduce its published 0.00149 ± 0.00010 / AF 0.938 from 2026-09-16 as a tight number. Fresh seeds 0, 1, 2 give 0.00147 ± 0.00019 and AF 0.929, so an absolute AF gate of 0.935 is failed by the record itself. Seed 0 was trained twice tonight in two fresh compiles: 0.00131 and 0.00135.
- Both research recipes lower gFVD on every seed. Both pay for it in the reward head: reward precision drops 5% and 8%. Head-gradient scaling at 0.1 explains the trade: the trunk learns less from reward, frames get better, reward precision gets worse.
- NorMuon itself is not the gain. The GPU-1 study of 2026-09-19 (`ablation_seed023.md`, seeds 0, 2, 3) measured it at +0.00003 against the baseline and 4% worse than original Muon inside the reduced recipe. The gain is shortcut training plus head-gradient control.
- Eval time differs by machine: 143 s here against 94 s on the research VM for the same 2048 windows, while cold training time matched within 1%. Only training time counts toward the budget.

Checkpoints of the record recipe, seeds 0, 1 and 2, SHA-256 prefixes fe322703, d748fbeb, cc9cec04, are published alongside the record row.
